# Studio Mini

**A pocket songwriting studio. Capture lyrics, audio, and video for a song idea in one place, before the idea disappears.**

As a songwriter, I'd have lyrics in my notes app, voice memos in another app, and video takes in my camera roll. Studio Mini puts all three on one dashboard.

## Features

- **Lyrics editor.** Full-screen writing with one-tap **verse / chorus / bridge / outro** section tags and a live word count. A preview shows on the dashboard.
- **Audio recorder.** Big tap-to-record button with a timer. Clips are saved and listed for playback.
- **Video recorder.** Live camera preview, a recording indicator, and saved clips (great for capturing a guitar part or performance idea).
- **Dashboard.** Tiles show your lyrics preview, a live camera thumbnail, and how many clips you've saved.

## Tech

Plain **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** with no frameworks. Recording uses the browser's built-in `getUserMedia` and `MediaRecorder` APIs.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in Chrome, or serve the folder locally, and allow microphone and camera access when asked.
